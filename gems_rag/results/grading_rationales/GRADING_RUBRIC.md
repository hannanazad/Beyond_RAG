# Per-response grading rubric

## Correctness

The score measures whether the response states the correct MUTCD conclusion,
rule, threshold, dimension, sign, table value, figure interpretation, or
answerability judgment.

## Completeness

The score measures whether the response includes all locked answer elements,
including conditions, exceptions, quantities, locations, subparts, and
normative distinctions.

## Faithfulness

The score measures whether the response remains supported by the locked
evidence and avoids unsupported, overstated, or source-inconsistent claims.

## Citation and evidence localization

The score measures whether the response identifies the correct MUTCD section,
table, figure, page, or other accepted evidence location.

## Strict full credit

A response earns strict full credit only when:

```text
correctness = 1.00
completeness = 1.00
faithfulness = 1.00
citation = 1.00
```

## Retrieval context

The rationale also reports the saved retrieval measures when available.
Retrieval results explain what evidence the system supplied to Claude Fable 5.
They do not change the four item-level answer grades stored in each response
record.
